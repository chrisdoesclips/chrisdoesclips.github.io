Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Chris Does Clips uses authorized social-platform connections to prepare, schedule, and publish short-form video content.

Information we process

When an account owner authorizes a platform connection, we may process the account identifier, display name, profile information, authorization tokens, publishing permissions, and media or captions the owner chooses to publish. We do not request or store the account password.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the connected account, prepare and publish requested content, display publishing status, and maintain the security and operation of the service.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the connected platform and service providers needed to operate the publishing workflow, or when required by law.

Retention and security

Authorization data is retained only while needed to keep an account connected and is protected using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Published content may remain on the destination platform according to that platform’s policies.

Your choices

You may revoke access through the connected platform’s settings. You may also request deletion of connection data by contacting Chris Does Clips through @chrisdoesclips on TikTok.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
